JesusSaves

JesusSaves is a modified build of NetSurf 3.11. The modifications (security and robustness fixes, static-library packaging, new name and logo) were made by the JesusSaves project in 2026.

JesusSaves exists thanks to the NetSurf Developers, whose work it is built on. NetSurf is free software under the GNU General Public Licence version 2, and so is JesusSaves. See about:changelog for what changed.
